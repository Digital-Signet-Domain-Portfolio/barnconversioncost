Hidden costs of barn conversion

Build-cost bands are the site's own indicative model, inflated to a Q2 2026 footing via the ONS Construction Output Price Index (April 2026). Class Q rules current to the 21 May 2024 GPDO amendment.

A barn conversion budget that quotes only £/m² shell-and-core misses £45,000-£110,000 of routinely-incurred ancillary spend. The audit-able ledger below itemises the lines.
