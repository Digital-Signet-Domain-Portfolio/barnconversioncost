Barn conversion 18-month cashflow

Build-cost bands are the site's own indicative model, inflated to a Q2 2026 footing via the ONS Construction Output Price Index (April 2026). Class Q rules current to the 21 May 2024 GPDO amendment.

A £500,000 barn conversion typically front-loads spend at months 3 (re-roof / shell), 9 (M&E first fix) and 14 (kitchen and final). Retention 5% holds back ~£25,000 to month 18+.

Retention

Standard JCT and JCT minor-works contracts hold back 5% retention, of which half (2.5%) releases at practical completion and the other half at end of defects (12 months later). Plan cashflow around the late release.
