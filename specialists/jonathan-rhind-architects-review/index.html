Jonathan Rhind Architects: barn conversion specialist review

Build-cost bands are the site's own indicative model, inflated to a Q2 2026 footing via the ONS Construction Output Price Index (April 2026). Class Q rules current to the 21 May 2024 GPDO amendment.

Jonathan Rhind Architects is a west country listed-building architect with deep portfolio of stone agricultural conversion in devon, somerset and cornwall. Coverage: Devon, Cornwall, Somerset, Dorset.

What they do

West Country listed-building architect with deep portfolio of stone agricultural conversion in Devon, Somerset and Cornwall. Verified against their published website Jonathan Rhind Architects as at 21 June 2026.

Fee model

Jonathan Rhind Architects does not publish a fee scale on its website. Engagement is quote-on-enquiry. We do not infer rates for quote-only firms; ask for a written fee scope before instructing.

When to instruct

  • Borderline Class Q where refusal risk is material and an experienced consultant adds win probability.
  • Full planning in a sensitive landscape (AONB, Green Belt, listed setting).
  • Multi-unit farmstead schemes inside the 10-dwelling / 1,000 m² cap.

When not

  • Simple Class Q on a sound single barn in an unrestricted area; a competent local architect may be enough.
  • Pure construction-cost questions; that is QS work, not planning consultancy.
