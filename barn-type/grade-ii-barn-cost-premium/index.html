Grade II barn cost premium

Build-cost bands are the site's own indicative model, inflated to a Q2 2026 footing via the ONS Construction Output Price Index (April 2026). Class Q rules current to the 21 May 2024 GPDO amendment.

A Grade II barn typically lands 18% to 25% above the equivalent non-listed cost. Conditions on consent often include lime-based build-up, breathable wall insulation and salvaged-where-possible joinery.

Typical Grade II conditions

  • Lime mortar, lime render, lime wash; no cement-based products on historic fabric.
  • Breathable wall build-up (wood-fibre / hemp lime); no foil-faced PIR on stone wall.
  • Salvage and re-use existing joinery; new windows in painted softwood or oak.
  • Retention of all existing trusses, with traditional repair rather than replacement.

£/m² impact

Wood-fibre wall insulation vs PIR
+£60-£120 / m² walling
Lime plaster vs gypsum
+£25-£60 / m² of plastered area
Oak truss repair vs steel replacement
varies, often 2-5x labour
Hand-made clay tile vs machine-made
+£40-£90 / m² of roof

VAT treatment

If the barn has never previously been residential, the conversion qualifies for the 5% reduced rate on labour under VAT Notice 708. The DIY housebuilders scheme (VAT431C) is available for materials reclaim if the converted dwelling is for your own occupation.
