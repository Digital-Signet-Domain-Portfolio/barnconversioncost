Modern portal-frame barn conversion

Build-cost bands are the site's own indicative model, inflated to a Q2 2026 footing via the ONS Construction Output Price Index (April 2026). Class Q rules current to the 21 May 2024 GPDO amendment.

Modern steel portal-frame barns scope at £1,750 to £2,250/m² mid spec. The clear-span structural advantage gives M&E layout freedom that stone barns cannot match. Class Q applies if the building was in agricultural use on 24 July 2023.

The structural advantage

A steel portal frame gives 12 to 24 metres of clear span. The conversion essentially wraps a new insulated envelope around the steel and slips a domestic plan inside, with no internal load-bearing walls forced on the layout. M&E first-fix is straightforward.

Class Q on portals

Class Q on a steel portal works if the building was put up for agricultural use and was in agricultural use on 24 July 2023. gov.uk Class Q guidance does not exclude steel structures; the same 150 m² per dwelling and 1,000 m² site caps apply.

Envelope choices

  • Insulated panel infill: aluzinc standing-seam outer, mineral wool / PIR composite, plasterboard inner. £180-£260 / m² of envelope.
  • Timber clad infill: ventilated rainscreen on breather membrane and frame. £210-£300 / m².
  • Brick or block skin: closer to a new-build cost; £250-£340 / m².
